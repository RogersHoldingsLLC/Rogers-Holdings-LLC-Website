M01 / INSIDE THE CABINET

The acoustic core.

Driver assemblies
Each cone mounts in a cast basket. A rigid brace ties the two chambers together and keeps the drivers seated.
Acoustic chambers
Felt lines the chambers. A foam gasket seals the joint around the front panel.
Power and wiring
A compact amplifier sits low in the cabinet. Clipped wire pairs run to each driver without crossing the moving assemblies.
