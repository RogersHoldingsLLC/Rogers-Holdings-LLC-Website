# Paired worksheet case screenshots
The six downloadable XLSX files in `portfolio/downloads` and the six paired example app routes are unchanged. Screenshots on their case pages are directly rendered excerpts of the published files or captured from the unchanged `?view=app` browser routes. The Form / Field and Silt & Ember storefront case studies reuse their companion pair and keep their standalone website still. All shown records come from the existing public sample workbooks and browser examples.
 
| Case | Desktop spreadsheet excerpt | Phone spreadsheet excerpt |

| Form / Field | Follow-up queue | Follow-up clock |
| Benchline Workshop | Production plan | Quick view |

Spreadsheet images are rendered from actual XLSX cells, including cached/calculated values and source styling, using the Artifact Tool spreadsheet renderer. They do not simulate Excel or Google Sheets chrome. Web app images are Chromium captures of the default sample state. The wide images add matching neutral margins without editing their contents; mobile variants ship separately. Full-size links open the static wide images. The six standalone app views retain their current routing, datasets and controls. In particular, Form / Field uses different sample quote records and demonstration dates in its two formats; no synchronization is implied.
