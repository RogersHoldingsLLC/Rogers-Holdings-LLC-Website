# Visual Storytelling Phase 1

This phase uses supplied Rogers Holdings deliverables and approved Eastland First Church of God project captures to make the work tangible without exposing private information or showing the internal Business Optimization Platform interface.

## Provenance and sanitization

- North Point Fitness is fictional demonstration material. Public derivatives are rendered from the supplied Digital Business Assessment and Improvement Plan PDFs.
- PDF footers containing Rogers Holdings contact details and dated document metadata are excluded from public derivatives. Each public North Point image carries an illustrative-example label.
- Eastland captures are supplied project visuals. Browser chrome, account controls, notifications, and unrelated interface areas are cropped before publication.
- Source PDFs and raw screenshots remain outside the public asset tree. Only optimized AVIF, WebP, and JPEG derivatives are published.

## Public proof structure

The homepage presents four assessment views (cover, executive briefing, key findings, roadmap), four improvement-plan views (cover, deliverables, timeline, next steps), and a factual Eastland journey from Facebook presence through search/profile discovery to an owned website experience.

Eastland website captures are presented in CSS-only MacBook and iPhone frames, with the real Facebook and Google Business Profile captures shown in restrained browser shells. The frames add context and hierarchy; they do not recreate or redraw the captured interfaces.

The Business Optimization Platform is mentioned only as Rogers Holdings' internal system for organizing evidence, priorities, and next actions. Its interface is not a public proof asset.

All proof images use intrinsic dimensions, responsive `picture` sources, lazy loading below the fold, and stable aspect ratios to prevent layout shift.
