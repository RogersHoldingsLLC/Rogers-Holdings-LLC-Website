How the sample follow-up rule works

Open records need both an owner and a next-contact date. Unassigned work comes first, then overdue, due today, undated and upcoming. The action depends on whether drawings are missing, a quote was sent or the customer is reviewing it. Won and lost quotes leave the open queue. The example no-contact record is locked out of follow-ups, even if its old date is overdue. Changing a date only plans work; it does not send a message or record a completed conversation. Counts include all owners and may overlap. The three pipeline counts include only open records. Select a pipeline stage again to clear its filter.
