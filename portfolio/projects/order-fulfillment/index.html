How the sample release rule works

“Ready” means packing is finished, not that an order may leave. Payment and the route-specific check must also be complete. Only then can you mark it “Collected / sent.” Pickup and shipping units are counted separately. Late open orders sort first, then blocked orders, clear-to-release orders and preparation. Counts cover all orders, not just the current filter. Completed orders must be reopened before their checks can change.
