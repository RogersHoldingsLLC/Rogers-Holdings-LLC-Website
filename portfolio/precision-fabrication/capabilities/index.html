BEFORE YOU REQUEST A QUOTE

What happens next?

Do I need a finished CAD file?

No. You can put together the request from a sketch, photo reference, or existing part. If a dimension is critical, include the measurement and say what it needs to fit.

How are price and lead time determined?

A shop confirms these after reviewing material, quantity, finish, and any special fit or inspection requirements. The builder prepares the request. It does not estimate a price or promise a delivery date.

Where does my request go?

Nowhere automatically. Copy the text or print it to PDF, then share it with the fabrication shop of your choice. This is a portfolio demonstration, not a live ordering service.
