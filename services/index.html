Websites & Refreshes

Make your website clearer and easier to use.

Launch a new site, fix a specific problem or improve the site you already have. Keep what works and focus on what visitors need next.

  • Pages & content: explain your services, show useful examples and make the next step clear.
  • Mobile experience: improve navigation, readability and contact paths on phones.
  • Focused refreshes: bring an existing page’s layout and message into better alignment.

Two starter options

Website Launch covers up to five core pages using your approved content and images, with contact paths, basic page titles and descriptions, launch checks and two revision rounds. Website + Custom Tracker adds an agreed website form and Google Sheet workflow. See the Website Creation page to compare the two options.

Existing-site repairs & refreshes

Tell us what needs to change and what should stay. We review the existing site and agree the specific pages, fixes, checks and handoff before work begins. Page copy, navigation and mobile usability can be part of that agreed work.

Light visual alignment, where useful

We can refine typography, color and page hierarchy around your existing identity so the site feels more consistent and the message is easier to follow. This is a focused visual refresh, not a standalone logo or full-branding package.

What you supply

Your approved business information, content and images, along with the contact steps you want visitors to take. Domain and hosting accounts stay under your control; handoff and account responsibilities are agreed as part of the scope.

Scope & separate costs

We agree the scope, written quote and timing before work begins. Domain registration, hosting and ongoing maintenance are separate. Writing, extra pages, migrations, booking/payment systems and custom functionality require separate quotes. Integrations outside the option you choose are quoted separately.

Simple Automation & Connected Tools

Fewer repeat steps. Clearer follow-up.

Connect a small, well-defined task across the tools you use. Forms and intake are one useful starting point, not the only kind of workflow we can review.

  • Collect the right details: clear questions, required fields and a response destination.
  • Reduce repeat entry: move agreed information into the tool where it is needed.
  • Make handoffs clear: define who reviews the result and what happens next.
Forms, automation & connection detailsIncluded work, requirements & quoteView detailsHide details

A concrete example: form to Custom Tracker

A website quote request can feed a Custom Tracker with the agreed request details, status and follow-up fields. The team then has one place to review the request and decide the next step. A request is not a confirmed order or booking.

Forms & intake

Review the questions, required fields, confirmation message and an agreed destination for responses. Build or improve the form, test the agreed paths and provide clear handoff instructions.

Start with one bounded workflow

We review the tools, available connections and access requirements before promising an automation. Agree the trigger, information moved, destination, checks and handoff before work begins. Complex integrations or ongoing operation are not assumed to be included.

What you supply

The form's purpose, the information you need, who handles responses and the platform you already use. Access and account responsibilities are arranged only after the scope is agreed.

Scope & quote

The number of questions, response destination and any requested integration affect the quote. We agree deliverables and a written quote after review, before paid work begins.
