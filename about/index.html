The person behind Rogers Holdings

I’m Brian Keith Rogers.

I’m a dad of two, I work on a farm, and I’m as comfortable getting my hands dirty as I am figuring out how a piece of technology works. Farm work has taught me to pay attention to what’s getting in the way and find a fix that holds up when the work gets busy. I bring that same approach to helping business owners.

I spent three years at a large freight brokerage, managing freight for 20 to 25 customers at a time. I quoted shipments, found carriers, negotiated rates, and coordinated pickups and deliveries. When a truck ran late or plans changed, I kept customers informed and worked out the next step. That work taught me to negotiate, prioritize competing deadlines, and stay accountable to customers when things got complicated. I bring that experience to the tools I build for business owners today.

I pay attention to the little things that make somebody’s day harder. When I built Eastland’s website, I wanted someone planning their first visit to find the service times and directions without hunting for them. When I helped with Missy’s workbook, I looked at what happens after a Facebook Live sale, when orders have to be totaled, packed, and sent out. I like figuring out where people get stuck and building something that helps them get on with the work.

My faith shapes how I work. I’ll give you a straight answer, help where I can, and follow through on what I say I’ll do. When you work with Rogers Holdings, you work directly with me.

You can build a website, set up a tracker, or connect your tools yourself. Keeping up with subscriptions, settings, and updates is another job. I’ll help you sort out what’s worth paying for and handle the setup and upkeep you’d rather hand off, so you can focus on running your business.
