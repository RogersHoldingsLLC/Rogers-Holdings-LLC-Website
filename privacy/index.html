Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

This policy explains how Rogers Holdings LLC (“Rogers Holdings,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information collected through rogersholdingsllc.com. It applies to the Business Snapshot form and other direct inquiries made through this website.

Information we collect

When you request a Business Snapshot, we may collect your first and last name, business name, email address, phone number, website URL, primary business challenge, optional notes, consent record, and basic submission details needed to process the request.

We also use Google Analytics to understand general website usage. Analytics may collect information such as device and browser type, pages visited, approximate location, referral source, and interaction data. We do not use the Business Snapshot form to request sensitive personal information.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review and respond to your request, communicate with you, evaluate whether our services may be a fit, maintain business records, protect the website, and improve our services and website experience. We do not sell your personal information.

How information is shared

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, receive form submissions, communicate with prospects, or analyze website performance. Those providers may use information only to perform services for us or as otherwise allowed by law. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, our business records, and legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the form and contact us directly instead. You may also ask us to access, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to legal and operational requirements. Browser settings and privacy tools may provide controls for cookies and analytics.

Third-party links

This website may link to websites operated by others. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Children’s privacy

This website and our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Business Snapshot form.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our website or practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Rogers Holdings LLC at briankeith@rogersholdingsllc.com or 859-404-7300.
