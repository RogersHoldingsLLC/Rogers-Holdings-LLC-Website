Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

This policy explains how Rogers Holdings LLC (“Rogers Holdings,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information collected through rogersholdingsllc.com. It applies to the Business Snapshot form and other direct inquiries made through this website.

Information we collect

When you request a Free Business Snapshot, we may collect your name, business name, email address, optional phone number, optional website URL, primary business challenge, and consent record. We also process operational metadata needed to secure and deliver the request, such as a request identifier, submission and processing timestamps, network and device information, and anti-abuse verification results.

We also use Google Analytics to understand general website usage. Analytics may collect information such as device and browser type, pages visited, approximate location, referral source, and interaction data. Do not submit passwords, payment information, government identification numbers, health information, or other sensitive personal information through the Business Snapshot form.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review your Free Business Snapshot request, prepare your Executive Brief, respond to your request, communicate about that request, evaluate whether our services may be a fit, maintain necessary business records, prevent abuse, protect the website, and improve our services and website experience. We do not sell your personal information.

How information is shared

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, receive form submissions, communicate with prospects, or analyze website performance. Those providers may use information only to perform services for us or as otherwise allowed by law. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

The Business Snapshot form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help identify automated or abusive activity. Cloudflare may process technical information such as network, device, browser, and interaction data for this security purpose. Turnstile is not used to make decisions about whether Rogers Holdings will work with you.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed to prepare and discuss the Executive Brief, manage the business relationship, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. We periodically review retained information and delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Security incidents

If we identify a security incident involving submitted information, we will investigate, take reasonable containment and remediation steps, and provide notices when required by applicable law.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the form and contact us directly instead. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete information you submitted, subject to legal and operational requirements. Contact permission for a Business Snapshot request is limited to reviewing and responding to that request; it is not consent to receive optional marketing. If we offer marketing communications, we will request separate optional consent and provide a way to unsubscribe. Browser settings and privacy tools may provide controls for cookies and analytics.

Third-party links

This website may link to websites operated by others. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

Children’s privacy

This website and our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Business Snapshot form.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as our website or practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Rogers Holdings LLC at briankeith@rogersholdingsllc.com or 859-404-7300.
