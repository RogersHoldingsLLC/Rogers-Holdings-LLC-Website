02 / Understand the boundary

A real practice would explain its enquiry and confidentiality terms before collecting information. This demonstration has no intake form, document upload or attorney-client relationship.

In a real service flow, the firm would confirm whether it can discuss the matter, explain any next steps and agree terms separately. No appointment, availability or representation is offered here.
